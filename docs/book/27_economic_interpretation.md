## Purpose

Translate raw model states into concise descriptions of current market structure while preserving uncertainty and avoiding a trading story.

## State card

For each raw state in [23_economic_interpretation.ipynb](../../23_economic_interpretation.ipynb), record:

1. ordered hierarchy shape and direction balance;
2. agreement, coherent frontier, or fragmentation;
3. scale ages and reversal progress;
4. DCC firing activity;
5. filtered confidence and persistence;
6. restart stability and any variance-floor warning;
7. transitions into and out of the state;
8. later activity or volatility differences, explicitly labeled diagnostic;
9. the stable distinction, if any, added beyond the direction-neutral and direction-aware baselines.

Training-only semantic names are annotations. Keep raw state IDs in every table so a convenient name cannot redefine the fitted object.

## Economic meaning

A useful description sounds like:

> The hierarchy is mostly coherent with opposition confirmed through the smaller scales, elevated small-scale firing, and moderate filtered confidence; the outer structure remains intact.

It does not sound like:

> Buy because this is the bullish state.

The state supplies context, not a position. Later realized activity can help explain the state but was unknown at the state timestamp.

## Price-path review

Inspect actual cases at low and high confidence, short and long dwell, and common transitions. A state whose numeric profile cannot be recognized on price may be statistically separable yet economically opaque.
